# Orbita Cloud — Plans and Pricing

Orbita Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Team and Enterprise.

- **Starter** costs US$ 0 per month. It includes 1 project, 5 GB of storage and community support only.
- **Team** costs US$ 29 per user per month, billed monthly, or US$ 290 per user per year when billed annually (two months free). It includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage per user and email support.
- **Enterprise** has custom pricing and requires a contract of at least 12 months. It includes SSO (SAML), audit logs, 1 TB of storage per user and a dedicated account manager.

Customers can upgrade at any time; the price difference is prorated to the day. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
