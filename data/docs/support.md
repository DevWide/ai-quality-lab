# Orbita Cloud — Support and SLA

Support channels by plan:
- Starter: community forum only.
- Team: email support, with a first response within 24 business hours.
- Enterprise: email and phone support 24/7, with a first response within 1 hour for critical incidents.

The uptime SLA is 99.9% for Team and 99.95% for Enterprise. If monthly uptime falls below the SLA, customers receive a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% below the target, up to 50%.

The Starter plan has no uptime SLA.
