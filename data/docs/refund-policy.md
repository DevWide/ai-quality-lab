# Orbita Cloud — Refund Policy

Monthly Team subscriptions can be refunded in full if the request is made within 14 days of the first payment. After 14 days, monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but the customer keeps access until the end of the paid period.

Annual Team subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of payment. After 30 days, Orbita Cloud refunds the unused months minus a 10% administrative fee.

Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms written in each contract.

Refunds are requested by email to billing@orbita.example and are processed within 7 business days to the original payment method.
